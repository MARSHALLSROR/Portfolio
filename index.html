04 · Questions

Before you ask

What is n8n?

A workflow automation tool that connects apps together, similar to Zapier or Make. It can run on n8n's own cloud or on your server.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You explain the task in plain words, and I handle the setup and show you how to use it.

What do I get for $15?

One simple workflow connecting up to three apps, such as a form that saves to a sheet and alerts your team. Bigger workflows get a clear quote first.

Tired of doing it by hand?

Send me a short description of the task. I reply in English or Arabic.
