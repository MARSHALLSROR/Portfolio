Who I Am

An AI engineer who builds deep learning models and scalable ELT pipelines — with an analytical edge from my background in accounting.

I'm an AI engineer with hands-on experience in machine learning, computer vision, NLP, and cloud data engineering. I build models that see, read, and reason — and the pipelines that bring them to production.

My work spans medical image classification with CNNs, real-time fire detection with YOLO, sentiment analysis with BERT, and abstractive summarization with BART. On the data side, I design modern ELT workflows using Snowflake, Azure, and dbt.

I also founded Sorour Brothers (as Abdo Sorour), where I write about AI, data, and automation. I believe in clean training pipelines, rigorous evaluation, and AI that ships.
